About

Designer.

Over a decade designing digital products across publishing, esports, and immersive entertainment. Currently Senior UX/UI Designer at Sandbox VR.

I'm a senior product designer originally from London, now based in Vancouver, BC. I've spent my career in varied and interesting worlds — national newspapers, competitive gaming platforms, and location-based VR — and the through-line has always been the same: make complex tasks simple to do.

I care about the details that most people don't notice — micro-interactions, information hierarchy, moments a user understands something without being told. That's the craft.

Currently at Sandbox VR leading design across product, brand, and in-venue experience.

Outside work, you can find me either skiing Whistler, playing some Magic: The Gathering, lifting or learning disc golf.
