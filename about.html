I'm a senior product designer originally from London UK, now based out of Vancouver, BC.

I've spent my career in national newspapers, competitive gaming platforms, location-based VR and the through-line has always been the same: make complex questions into simple answers.

I care about craft, efficacy and, most importantly yet often forgotten, making the whole product a joy to use.

Outside work, you can find me dog sitting, lifting heavy, playing Magic: The Gathering, skiing or scoring consistent double bogeys in disc golf at Queen Elizabeth Park.
