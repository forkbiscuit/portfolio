About

Designer.
From London.
Now Vancouver.

Over a decade designing digital products across publishing, esports, and immersive entertainment. Currently Senior UX/UI Designer at Sandbox VR.

I'm a senior product designer originally from London, now based in Vancouver, BC with my partner Sarah Jane. I've spent my career in varied and interesting worlds — national newspapers, competitive gaming platforms, and location-based VR — and the through-line has always been the same: make complex things feel effortless.

I care about the details that most people don't notice — the micro-interactions, the information hierarchy, the moment a user understands something without being told. That's the craft.

Currently at Sandbox VR leading design across product, brand, and in-venue experience. Before that: Battlefy, FACEIT, The Telegraph, Evening Standard, and The Independent.

Outside work: skiing Whistler, kayaking the inlet, building proxy Commander decks, and learning to DJ on a Pioneer DDJ-FLX4.
